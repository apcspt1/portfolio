<h2 class="ocs__section-title">Volunteer Roles</h2>
<p class="ocs__description">Explore the different volunteer roles and groups.</p>

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--card cols-2">
<div class="ocs__grid-cell">
<h3 class="ocs__hub-card-title">Research &amp; Education</h3>
<ul><li>Research mental health topics relevant to monthly themes.</li><li>Write blog posts for the MGB website.</li><li>Create infographics.</li><li>Assist in creating online resources and educational materials.</li><li>Create art activities for the Tool Kit Program.</li><li>Collaborate with other volunteers to boost our impact.</li></ul>
</div>
<div class="ocs__grid-cell">
<h3 class="ocs__hub-card-title">Webmaster</h3>
<ul><li>Update and maintain the MGB website, ensuring all information is current and accurate.</li><li>Enhance the user experience with a visually appealing and user-friendly layout.</li><li>Work with the Social Media &amp; Marketing Team to align web content with campaigns and events.</li><li>Monitor website analytics and report on visitor engagement and traffic trends.</li></ul>
</div>
<div class="ocs__grid-cell">
<h3 class="ocs__hub-card-title">Tool Kit Assembly Leader</h3>
<ul><li>Lead a team of volunteers during assembly, fostering a positive and collaborative environment.</li><li>Coordinate tasks such as assembling kits, quality checks, and packing for delivery.</li><li>Organize Tool Kit materials, including books, art supplies, and packaging.</li><li>Coordinate with school partners to ensure Tool Kits are distributed on time.</li></ul>
</div>
<div class="ocs__grid-cell">
<h3 class="ocs__hub-card-title">Community Outreach &amp; Events</h3>
<ul><li>Assist with in-person art events in San Diego, California.</li><li>Help prepare and deliver resources, including art therapy Tool Kits.</li><li>Identify opportunities to increase awareness of MGB.</li><li>Collaborate with other volunteers to boost our impact.</li></ul>
</div>
<div class="ocs__grid-cell">
<h3 class="ocs__hub-card-title">Video Content Creator</h3>
<ul><li>Create instructional arts and crafts videos and Kids At Heart video content for social media.</li><li>Post regularly across social platforms, including Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.</li><li>Increase engagement with educational content and the website.</li><li>Help with fundraiser events that support MGB programs.</li></ul>
</div>
</div>
