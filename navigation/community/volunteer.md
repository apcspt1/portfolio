### Volunteer Roles
* Learn about our different volunteer roles and groups

| Role | Description |
|---|---:|
| Research & Education | * Reasearch mental health topics relevent to monthly themes. * Write blog posts for the MGB website. * Create infographics. * Assist in creation of online resources and educational materials. * Create art activities for the Tool Kit Program. * Work collaboratively with other volunteers to boost our impact. |
| Webmaster | - Update and maintain MGB Website, ensuring all information is current and accurate. - Enhance the user experience by maintaining a visually appealing and user-friendly layout. - Work closely with the Social Media & Marketing Team to align web content with ongoing campaigns and events. - Monitor website analytics and provide reports on visitor engagement and traffic trends. |
| Tool Kit Assembly leader | - Lead a team of volunteers during assembly, fostering a positive and collaborative environment. - Coordinate volunteer tasks, assigning roles such as assembling kits, quality checks, and packing for delivery. - Ensure all materials for Tool Kits (books, art supplies, packaging) are organized and ready prior to assembly day. - Coordinate with school partners to ensure Tool Kits are distributed on time. |
| Community Outreach & Events | - Assist and participate in our in-person art events in San Diego, CA. - Hands-on support in preparing and delivering resources, including art therapy tool kits. - Actively search for opportunities to increase audience awareness about MGB. - Work collaboratively with other volunteers to boost our impact. |
| Video Content Creator | - Creating visual assets (instructional arts and crafts videos, Kids At Heart Video series) for social media. - Make regular posts for various social platforms (IG, Facebook, TikTok). - Increase social media engagement on platforms, educational content, and website. - Helping with fundraiser events to support programs. |
