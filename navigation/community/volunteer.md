### Volunteer Roles
* Learn about our different volunteer roles and groups

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| Research & Education | &bull; Research mental health topics relevant to monthly themes. <br> &bull; Write blog posts for the MGB website. <br> &bull; Create infographics. <br> &bull; Assist in creation of online resources and educational materials. <br> &bull; Create art activities for the Tool Kit Program. <br> &bull; Work collaboratively with other volunteers to boost our impact. |
| Webmaster | &bull; Update and maintain MGB Website, ensuring all information is current and accurate. <br> &bull; Enhance the user experience by maintaining a visually appealing and user-friendly layout. <br> &bull; Work closely with the Social Media & Marketing Team to align web content with ongoing campaigns and events. <br> &bull; Monitor website analytics and provide reports on visitor engagement and traffic trends. |
| Tool Kit Assembly leader | &bull; Lead a team of volunteers during assembly, fostering a positive and collaborative environment. <br> &bull; Coordinate volunteer tasks, assigning roles such as assembling kits, quality checks, and packing for delivery. <br> &bull; Ensure all materials for Tool Kits (books, art supplies, packaging) are organized and ready prior to assembly day. <br> &bull; Coordinate with school partners to ensure Tool Kits are distributed on time. |
| Community Outreach & Events | &bull; Assist and participate in our in-person art events in San Diego, CA. <br> &bull; Hands-on support in preparing and delivering resources, including art therapy tool kits. <br> &bull; Actively search for opportunities to increase audience awareness about MGB. <br> &bull; Work collaboratively with other volunteers to boost our impact. |
| Video Content Creator | &bull; Create visual assets (instructional arts and crafts videos, Kids At Heart Video series) for social media. <br> &bull; Make regular posts for various social platforms (IG, Facebook, TikTok). <br> &bull; Increase social media engagement on platforms, educational content, and website. <br> &bull; Help with fundraiser events to support programs. |
