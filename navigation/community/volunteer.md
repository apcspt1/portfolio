### Volunteer Roles
* Learn about our different volunteer roles and groups

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| Research & Education | • Research mental health topics relevant to monthly themes.<br>• Write blog posts for the MGB website.<br>• Create infographics.<br>• Assist in creation of online resources and educational materials.<br>• Create art activities for the Tool Kit Program.<br>• Work collaboratively with other volunteers to boost our impact. |
| Webmaster | • Update and maintain MGB Website, ensuring all information is current and accurate.<br>• Enhance the user experience by maintaining a visually appealing and user-friendly layout.<br>• Work closely with the Social Media & Marketing Team to align web content with ongoing campaigns and events.<br>• Monitor website analytics and provide reports on visitor engagement and traffic trends. |
| Tool Kit Assembly leader | • Lead a team of volunteers during assembly, fostering a positive and collaborative environment.<br>• Coordinate volunteer tasks, assigning roles such as assembling kits, quality checks, and packing for delivery.<br>• Ensure all materials for Tool Kits (books, art supplies, packaging) are organized and ready prior to assembly day.<br>• Coordinate with school partners to ensure Tool Kits are distributed on time. |
| Community Outreach & Events | • Assist and participate in our in-person art events in San Diego, CA.<br>• Hands-on support in preparing and delivering resources, including art therapy tool kits.<br>• Actively search for opportunities to increase audience awareness about MGB.<br>• Work collaboratively with other volunteers to boost our impact. |
| Video Content Creator | • Create visual assets (instructional arts and crafts videos, Kids At Heart Video series) for social media.<br>• Make regular posts for various social platforms (IG, Facebook, TikTok).<br>• Increase social media engagement on platforms, educational content, and website.<br>• Help with fundraiser events to support programs. |
